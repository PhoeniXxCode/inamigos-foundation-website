About InAmigos

Founded on September 23, 2020, InAmigos Foundation is a registered NGO committed to uniting minds for lasting social change.

  • 80G & 12A Certified
  • CSR-1 Registered
  • Transparent & Impact-Driven
  • Working across 6 core sectors

We believe in empowering communities through education, health, environment, and skill development.
